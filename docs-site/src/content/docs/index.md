Keep creative history usable by humans and agents. Lineage is a local-first
visual workspace for creating, reviewing, and continuing agent-assisted
creative work.

## Choose your path

### Evaluate Lineage

Understand the product’s shared-state model, traceable creative history, and
temporary agent claims in [What is Lineage?](start-here/what-is-lineage).

### Get started

Use a verified runtime channel and a named profile with
[Installation and first run](start-here/installation-first-run), then
[create your first workspace](start-here/first-workspace).

### Use Lineage

Learn when to [branch or re-roll](concepts/branches-vs-rerolls), how
[attempt history](concepts/attempts-current-version) works, and how to
[review and approve assets](workflows/review-approve-assets).

### Operate and integrate

Review [integration maturity](integrations/index), protect
[local-first data](operations/local-first-data), and verify
[profiles and database identity](operations/profiles-database-identity).
