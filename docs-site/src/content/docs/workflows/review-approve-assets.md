## What this does

Review turns visual judgment into durable state. Approval, rejection,
annotations, and selections remain available to later sessions.

## Step-by-step workflow

1. Open **Review** and choose the relevant queue or workspace.
2. Compare candidates at a useful size.
3. Inspect attempt history when similar candidates share an asset identity.
4. Add concise annotations describing what works or needs to change.
5. Approve the useful result or reject it with a reason.
6. Select approved inputs for the next variation or backup.

Approval is not publication. Social scheduling remains a separate preview
workflow, and cloud backup requires explicit provider configuration.
